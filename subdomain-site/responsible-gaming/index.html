Responsible Gaming: Set Limits and Stay in Control

Games should remain entertainment. This guide covers simple ways to set limits, recognize when play is becoming difficult and take a break.

Decide your limits before a session

Choose a time limit and a spending limit before opening a game. Treat the amount as entertainment spending that you can afford to lose, and stop when the limit is reached. Do not borrow money, use essential bills or chase losses.

A break between sessions can help you make decisions calmly. Avoid playing when you feel upset, pressured, tired or under the influence of alcohol or other substances.

Notice changes in your habits

Warning signs can include spending more time or money than intended, hiding play from people close to you, feeling unable to stop, or allowing gaming to affect sleep, work, studies or relationships.

If you notice these patterns, pause access and talk with someone you trust. Consider contacting a qualified local support service that understands gambling-related harm.

Use available account controls

If the service offers deposit, time, cooling-off or self-exclusion controls, review how they work before you need them. These controls differ by provider and location, so check the current terms and support information.

Uninstalling an app, muting promotional messages or blocking access on your device can also create space while you decide what support would help.

Protect younger people

Online gaming and gambling services are not suitable for people below the legal age. Keep payment details and account credentials private, use device controls where appropriate and discuss online spending with young people in your care.

If someone may be at immediate risk of harm, contact local emergency or professional support services.

Quick answers

Responsible Gaming: Set Limits and Stay in Control FAQs

What does responsible gaming mean?

It means keeping play within limits, understanding the risks and stopping when it is no longer enjoyable or manageable.

What should I do if I keep exceeding my limits?

Pause play, use any available account controls and speak with a trusted person or qualified local support service.

Can gaming be a reliable way to make money?

No. Outcomes are uncertain; do not treat gaming as income or a way to recover losses.

Are online games appropriate for minors?

No. Follow legal age requirements and keep access away from people below the applicable age.
