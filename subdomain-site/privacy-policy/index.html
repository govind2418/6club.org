Information and site operation

This project is a public information website. The guide pages do not ask visitors to submit account passwords, payment details or one-time codes. Hosting providers may process basic technical data, such as request and device information, to deliver and protect the site.

If analytics, contact forms or other data collection are added later, this policy should be updated to describe what is collected and how it is used.

Cookies and third-party links

The website may rely on essential hosting or security technologies. A linked external website may use its own cookies and data practices; review its privacy information before interacting with it.

This site does not control the privacy practices of external services.

Your choices

You can manage cookies through your browser settings and choose whether to follow external links. Avoid sharing sensitive account information on a public device or through an unverified page.

For account data held by a separate gaming service, contact that service directly through a verified channel.
